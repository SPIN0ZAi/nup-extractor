Simple Workflow

How It Works

Zero installation or external runtimes required—ready in seconds from a single ~5.5 MB executable.

1. Drag & Drop to Extract

Drag one or multiple .nup files directly onto NUP_Extractor.exe (or onto the app window) to automatically extract Base games, Updates, and DLCs next to each archive.

2. Inspect & Verify First

Launch the app, click anywhere on the dropzone, or double-click a registered .nup file to explore Title/Build IDs, search files, verify SHA-256 hashes, or extract specific folders.

3. Pack Game Folders

Switch to Pack mode, drop or select a folder containing your game files (Base, Updates, & DLCs), pick Fast, Ultra, or RAW compression, and build a verified .nup archive.
